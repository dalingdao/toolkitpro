1. What is a QR Code?

QR codes (Quick Response codes) are 2D barcodes that store data in a grid of black and white squares. They can be scanned by smartphones to instantly open URLs, connect to WiFi, or display text.

QR codes are essential for:

  • Marketing - linking print materials to digital content
  • Restaurants - digital menus
  • Events - tickets and check-in
  • Payments - mobile payment links (WeChat, Alipay)
  • Business cards - vCard contact info

3. QR Code Types

QR codes can encode different types of data:

  • URL - opens a website when scanned
  • WiFi - auto-connects to a network
  • Text - displays plain text
  • Email - opens an email draft
  • Phone - dials a number
  • vCard - saves contact information

4. Custom QR Codes

Modern QR generators let you customize:

  • Colors - foreground and background
  • Size - resolution for print or screen
  • Logo - embed a logo in the center
  • Error correction - higher level allows logo placement

Important: Keep enough contrast (dark on light) for reliable scanning. Avoid inverting colors.

5. Best Practices

  • Use high error correction (H) if embedding a logo
  • Generate at 500+ px for print quality
  • Keep quiet zone (margin) around the code
  • Test scan before publishing
  • Avoid low contrast or busy backgrounds
